A family catch-up, an office celebration or friends dropping by: Diwali plans come in different sizes. Start with the people you are inviting and the kind of food break you want. Then ask the kitchen about your date before promising a menu to everyone.

## When is Diwali in 2026?

Diwali falls on **8 November 2026**, according to [Drik Panchang's Diwali calendar](https://www.drikpanchang.com/hindu-festivals/diwali/diwali.html?year=2026). Family observances and workplace celebrations may happen on different days. Put your actual gathering date in the enquiry, even if it is before or after Diwali.

## A simple shortlist for your celebration

For a mixed group of regular vegetarian eaters, compare [pizzas](../../menu/category/pizza/) and [grilled sandwiches](../../menu/category/sandwiches/) first. Read each filling description and share the dish-page links with the people choosing food. Our menu photographs help you see the options without sharing private recipes.

[Fries and snacks](../../menu/category/snacks/) can be another starting point. If your group prefers bowls, explore [Maggi](../../menu/category/maggi/) and [pasta](../../menu/category/pasta/). These are menu ideas, not a fixed Diwali package or a promise of bulk availability. We do not currently publish a confirmed festival combo or festival discount here.

## What to put in a party or bulk-order enquiry

Choose Party / bulk order enquiry on the [contact form](../../contact/?type=party), then select the closest occasion or Other celebration. Include:

- The actual date and the time you hope to serve food.
- An approximate guest count, including any children.
- The dishes or categories your group is considering.
- Food allergies or restrictions that need discussion.
- Your name, email and required phone number for follow-up.

For an office celebration, mention whether you need separate portions or have other serving requirements. For a family gathering, mention whether you are still finalising the guest count. The kitchen will discuss what it can accommodate; do not treat a submitted enquiry as a confirmed reservation or order.

## Regular orders and personalised offers

For individual orders, open our Zomato menu using the website's order button. Zomato shows current prices, item availability, delivery eligibility and any offers available to your account. We cannot promise the same offer to every customer or guarantee a festival-day delivery time.

All our menu dishes are pure veg and egg-free. That does not make them free from every allergen or suitable for every special diet. Ask before agreeing on food for guests with specific requirements.
