A birthday, an anniversary or a college get-together starts with a little planning. If you want to ask Bhuk Lagla about food for a gathering in Sundargarh, our [party enquiry form](../../contact/?type=party) is a place to begin.

The useful starting information is the occasion, date, approximate number of guests and the dishes you are considering. The kitchen needs to confirm what is possible before an enquiry becomes an arrangement.

## Tell us the occasion

Choose birthday, anniversary, college or school gathering, office gathering, farewell or another celebration. A short description helps us understand what you have in mind.

## Include your date and approximate guest count

Add the planned date and approximate number of guests. Mention whether you are still choosing a date. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a slot or guarantee that we can accommodate an event.

## Share food preferences

Browse the [menu](../../menu/) for inspiration and mention dishes or categories you are interested in. If anyone has an allergy or dietary restriction, ask about it before agreeing to any arrangements. Our dishes are pure veg and egg-free, but that does not cover every dietary need.

## Wait for confirmation

The kitchen will reply by email about what is possible. An enquiry is not an order or a payment request. For ordinary individual orders, use our Zomato link and check current delivery availability on the platform.

## A checklist you can use

1. Choose **Party / bulk order enquiry** on the contact page.
2. Add your name, email and phone so the kitchen can reply.
3. Select the occasion and add the planned date and approximate guest count.
4. Say whether food is intended as a snack or a meal, and list any dishes you are considering.
5. Mention ingredient restrictions as questions to confirm, rather than assuming the regular menu meets them.
6. Send the enquiry and wait for a reply about availability and arrangements. Do not travel, pay or assume a slot is reserved based only on the form submission.

## Example enquiry: an office gathering

“We are considering food for an office get-together on [date], for approximately [guest count] people. It will be a snack break. We are interested in grilled sandwiches and fries. Please confirm the available options, quantities, arrangements and any ingredient questions before we decide.”

This is an illustrative message template, not a completed event or a customer success story. Replace the placeholders with your plans; do not include payment details or sensitive personal information.

## How many items should I request?

There is no fixed pieces-per-guest rule on this website. Appetite, other food being served and the portion options available at the time all matter. Tell the kitchen the context and ask for confirmation. The menu illustrations are not a portion-measuring guide.
