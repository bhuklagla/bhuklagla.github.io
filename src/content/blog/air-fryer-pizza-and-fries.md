Our menu uses different cooking methods for different dishes. Pizza and fries at Bhuk Lagla Kitchen are cooked in an air fryer; sandwiches are grilled. Knowing that difference makes the menu easier to understand.

## Pizza from the air fryer

Our [pizzas](../../menu/category/pizza/) use an air-fryer finish. Pick a topping that matches your craving, then open the dish page for the public description and photograph.

## Fries for a snack break

Our [fries](../../menu/category/snacks/) are air-fryer cooked with a light oil drizzle rather than deep-fried. That does not make them oil-free, and we do not describe the whole menu that way.

## Grilled sandwiches and bowls

[Sandwiches](../../menu/category/sandwiches/) are grilled. Pasta and Maggi have their own cooking methods. We keep the menu descriptions specific to each dish instead of applying one claim to everything we serve.

## Choose what works for you

All menu dishes are pure veg and egg-free. If you have a food allergy or a dietary restriction, [contact the kitchen](../../contact/) before making arrangements. Vegetarian or egg-free does not mean free from other allergens, and shared preparation equipment may matter to you.
