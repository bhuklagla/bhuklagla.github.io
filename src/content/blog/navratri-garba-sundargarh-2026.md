## When is Navratri in 2026?

Shardiya Navratri begins on **11 October 2026**, with Dussehra on **20 October 2026**. These dates are listed in [Drik Panchang's 2026 Hindu calendar](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?year=2026). Local puja observances and event schedules should be checked with the relevant organiser; festival dates do not confirm that a Garba event is taking place.

Recheck the organiser's announcement before travelling.
