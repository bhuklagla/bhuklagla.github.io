Durga Puja evenings can mean meeting friends, spending time with family and heading out to see the celebrations. Give your food plans the same thought as your outing: choose what everyone likes, check when you need it and leave time to confirm availability.

## Dussehra 2026: the date to keep in mind

Dussehra, also called Vijayadashami or Dasara, falls on **20 October 2026** in the [2026 calendar published by Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?year=2026). Durga Puja programmes and individual rituals follow their own local schedules. Check the announcement from your chosen puja committee rather than treating a general festival calendar as a Sundargarh pandal timetable.

This guide is for food planning. We have not verified local pandal opening times, procession routes, traffic restrictions or late-night programmes. Check organisers' current updates before travelling and avoid relying on last year's poster.

## Choose a food break that suits your group

Start with our [pure-veg pizza selection](../../menu/category/pizza/). Paneer Chataka Pizza is one option to explore; its dish page has the photograph and public description. If a grilled bite sounds better, compare the fillings in our [sandwich menu](../../menu/category/sandwiches/).

For a smaller snack break, look at [fries and snacks](../../menu/category/snacks/). A bowl of [Maggi](../../menu/category/maggi/) or [pasta](../../menu/category/pasta/) gives you another direction. Different cravings do not have to mean guessing: each dish has its own menu page.

Our food is pure veg and egg-free. Pizza and fries are air-fryer cooked; fries use a light oil drizzle. Sandwiches are grilled. These are specific cooking descriptions, not a claim that every dish is oil-free or suitable for every dietary restriction.

## Check Zomato before counting on delivery

Choose your actual delivery location on Zomato and check current item availability, delivery estimates and eligible offers there. A website photograph does not mean an item is available at that moment, and festival-day delivery times or serviceability should not be assumed. We do not promise delivery to pandals or event venues.
