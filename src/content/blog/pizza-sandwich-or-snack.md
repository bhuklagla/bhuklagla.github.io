Sometimes the hardest part of a food break is deciding what sounds good. At Bhuk Lagla Kitchen in Sundargarh, you can browse the food before opening Zomato. Here are a few places to start.

## In a pizza mood?

Browse our [pizza menu](../../menu/category/pizza/). Paneer Chataka Pizza brings together paneer and onion. If you prefer a simpler topping, explore the other pizzas and open each dish to read its description. Our pizzas are cooked in an air fryer.

## Want a grilled bite?

A [grilled sandwich](../../menu/category/sandwiches/) is a familiar choice for a study break or an evening snack. Read the filling descriptions to pick what you like: corn, paneer and other vegetarian combinations appear across the sandwich menu.

## More of a snack break?

Have a look at [fries and snacks](../../menu/category/snacks/). Or browse [Maggi](../../menu/category/maggi/) and [pasta](../../menu/category/pasta/) if a bowl sounds better. All our dishes are pure veg and egg-free.

## Check before ordering

The website helps you choose. Zomato shows current prices, availability, delivery options and offers eligible for your account. An item appearing here does not guarantee that it is available on the platform at that moment.
