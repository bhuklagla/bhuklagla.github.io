Choose pizza if you want to compare toppings on an 8-inch base, a grilled sandwich if you want to choose a bread filling, or Maggi and pasta if you want a bowl. Fries are the two seasoning-led snack choices on Bhuk Lagla’s menu. This guide explains those menu differences; it does not rank dishes by popularity, price or nutrition.

## Pizza or a grilled sandwich: what changes?

Our pizzas are described as 8-inch and air-fryer cooked. The choices vary by topping and sauce: for example, Paneer Chataka Pizza has paneer and onion, while Double Veg Pizza has onion and capsicum. Schezwan Veg Pizza uses those vegetables with a Schezwan kick.

Sandwiches are grilled and vary by filling. Creamy Veg Grilled Sandwich lists onion, capsicum and creamy mayo. Veg Cheese Slice Grilled Sandwich adds a cheese slice to that combination. Corn Cream Grilled Sandwich and its Cheese Slice counterpart have a different corn-and-cheese filling.

The practical question is whether you want a pizza topping or a grilled filling. Neither format alone tells you how many people it will satisfy. Open the [side-by-side menu guide](../../choose/) to compare the named ingredients.

## In a pizza mood?

Browse our [pizza menu](../../menu/category/pizza/). Paneer Chataka Pizza brings together paneer and onion. If you prefer a simpler topping, explore the other pizzas and open each dish to read its description. Our pizzas are cooked in an air fryer.

For a corn comparison, start with [Corn Masti Pizza](../../menu/corn-masti-pizza/), [Corn Olive Pizza](../../menu/corn-olive-pizza/) and [Schezwan Corn Pizza](../../menu/schezwan-corn-pizza/). Corn Masti lists corn with pizza sauce; Corn Olive adds black olive; Schezwan Corn lists red paprika and a spicy Schezwan sauce. They are three different ingredient combinations, not mild/medium/hot ratings.

## Want a grilled bite?

A [grilled sandwich](../../menu/category/sandwiches/) is a familiar choice for a study break or an evening snack. Read the filling descriptions to pick what you like: corn, paneer and other vegetarian combinations appear across the sandwich menu.

## More of a snack break?

Have a look at [fries and snacks](../../menu/category/snacks/). Or browse [Maggi](../../menu/category/maggi/) and [pasta](../../menu/category/pasta/) if a bowl sounds better. All our dishes are pure veg and egg-free.

## Red sauce pasta or white sauce pasta?

[Chatpata Red Sauce Pasta](../../menu/chatpata-red-sauce-pasta/) is penne with tangy red sauce, onion, capsicum and herbs. [Creamy White Sauce Pasta](../../menu/creamy-white-sauce-pasta/) is fusilli with creamy white sauce, sweet corn and herbs. The pasta shape and vegetables change along with the sauce. Check Zomato for the portion options currently offered.

## An example of choosing for two different preferences

If one person wants paneer and another wants corn, start with the ingredient descriptions rather than assuming both need the same dish. Paneer appears in Paneer Chataka Pizza and Paneer Tandoori Grilled Sandwich; corn appears across pizza, sandwiches and white sauce pasta. This is a planning example, not a report of a customer order or a recommendation of serving quantities.
