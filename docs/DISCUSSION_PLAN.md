# Discussion plan

Discuss one topic at a time. Record each decision in `WEBSITE_BRIEF.md`, with any unresolved detail explicitly marked. The user controls the pace; this checklist is not permission to implement.

The primary customer journey is already confirmed: browse the menu and order on Zomato. The working website name is Bhuk Lagla Kitchen, using the user's wording. The brand spelling is Bhuk Lagla.

## Topics in order

1. **Audience and brand promise:** what customers should remember, the main audience, and how the affordable proposition should feel.
2. **Launch goals and success measures:** desired customer actions and how to judge whether the site helps the business.
3. **Features and launch scope:** essentials, useful later additions, and explicit exclusions. Discuss menu categories, search/filtering, item details, availability and offers without assuming all are needed.
4. **Customer journey:** entry points, browsing behaviour, Zomato handoff, order-button placement and closed/unavailable states.
5. **Pages and navigation:** homepage, menu, about, contact, FAQs, policies or other pages only where useful; one-page versus multiple-page structure.
6. **Visual design:** approved logo and brand palette, typography, layout, food photography, icons, animation, mobile behaviour and accessibility.
7. **Content and business facts:** languages, tone, descriptions, current menu/prices, images, hours, address, contact information and verified Zomato link.
8. **Technology stack:** compare current supported options against the agreed requirements, maintenance needs and costs; verify current documentation before selecting versions.
9. **Architecture and data:** rendering approach, shared components, menu/config source, content editing, image pipeline and integrations; add backend/storage only when a requirement needs them.
10. **SEO, sharing and measurement:** local search, metadata, structured data, social previews, optional analytics and privacy implications.
11. **Hosting and operations:** domain, deployment, environments, ongoing costs, backups, access and who maintains content.
12. **Quality and acceptance:** performance, accessibility, device/browser coverage, security, accurate facts and checks proving the launch requirements are met.
13. **PRD and implementation phases:** consolidate agreed decisions into a PRD with scope, requirements, architecture, data/content needs, acceptance criteria, dependencies and rollout steps. Review remaining open questions before starting the first implementation phase.
