# Food and brand assets

Inspect the workspace brand handoff and approved assets before making derivatives. Preserve original files and record each derivative's source. A raster logo or SVG containing raster pixels is not an editable vector master.

Use Bhuk Lagla's own realistic food images. Match the described ingredients, serving and approved photographic treatment. Keep the complete dish sharp, centred and inside the frame with useful margins. Agree on the website's photo treatment during discussion rather than inheriting Oven Vibe's black backdrop or top-down requirement.

Preserve full-resolution masters. Make web derivatives with sensible dimensions, compression and supported formats, and reserve image space to prevent layout movement. Do not upscale a soft image and call it a higher-quality master. For Zomato exports, separately follow the approved 1800 x 1200, under-10-MB requirement when requested; web derivatives need not have those dimensions.

When generating or editing images, read the session's imagegen skill and use its supported image tool. Check each result visually against the specific menu item before accepting it.

After implementation, verify crops on menu cards, mobile pages and share previews. Source examples: `skills/references/oven-vibe/take-a-food-photo.md`, `manage-menu-photos.md`, and `update-item-photo.md`. Their photo scripts and fixed background are source-project dependencies, not installed here.
